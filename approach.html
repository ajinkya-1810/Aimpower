Our Approach & Methodology

Rights-based development, gender-responsive programming, and community-led action.

AIMPOWER FOUNDATION’s methodology is rooted in Rights-Based Development, Gender-Responsive Programming, and Community-Led Action. We do not work for communities — we work with them. Every programme is co-designed with local stakeholders to ensure cultural relevance, community ownership, and lasting impact.

Gender Sensitization & Leadership Building

At the heart of our work is a transformative vision: to build a society where gender equality is not just a policy goal, but a lived reality. We work simultaneously at three levels:

At the Individual Level
  • Building critical consciousness on gender, patriarchy, and feminist thought
  • Developing leadership skills among women and girls
  • Empowering youth as gender justice champions and change-makers
  • Fostering positive masculinity and inclusive behaviour change
At the Institutional Level
  • Training Internal Complaints Committees (ICCs) for POSH Act 2013 compliance
  • Gender audits of academic institutions and workplaces
  • Integrating gender perspectives into social work education curricula
  • Building gender-sensitive HR practices in partner organisations
At the Community Level
  • Community-led gender dialogues in rural Vidarbha villages
  • Engaging men and boys as partners in gender equality
  • Strengthening women’s collective voice through SHGs and enterprise groups
  • Linking women to legal entitlements, welfare schemes, and economic opportunities

Long-Term Vision — Communities where women and girls exercise equal rights, lead with confidence, and make decisions free from violence, discrimination, and structural barriers.
